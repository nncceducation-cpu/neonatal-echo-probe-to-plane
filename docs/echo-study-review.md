# Supplied echo study integration

Source: user-authorized Cardiac cases Drive folder
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1is6Od1Hse74lcVZwOvI82Fgjobxi0oP1

Reviewed local frames at 0, 0.5 and 1 seconds for five short cine loops.
These show cardiac-cycle motion, not a calibrated base-to-apex probe sweep.
The mappings are educational visual-review assignments, not clinical validation.

| Preset | Original clip | Basis / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| PLAX | Heart Normal - Case 1 - LA.mp4 | LV long axis, mitral and aortic-root continuity |
| PSAX aortic | Heart Normal - Case 1 - SA.mp4 | Central aortic-root cross-section |
| PSAX mitral | ShortAxis.avi | Moving central valve apparatus; level inference needs clinician confirmation |
| PSAX papillary | Heart Normal - Case 1 - SAB.mp4 | Ventricular section and muscle profiles; level inference needs clinician confirmation |
| Apical four chamber | Heart Normal - Case 1 - Chambers.mp4 | Four-chamber view; source apex at bottom, retained without altering source pixels |

View terminology cross-checked against ASE pediatric TTE guidance:
https://www.asecho.org/guideline/comprehensive-pediatric-tte/

No source is assigned to apical PSAX, subcostal or suprasternal presets without
a reviewed matching clip. Pathology-named studies were not substituted as
normal references. Source clips are separate patients from the mesh, not an
exact 2D/3D registration. Playback time must not be equated to slice angle.

References are unavailable after >1 degree pose deviation; resetting restores
the preset clip. Source files remain unmodified (ShortAxis AVI is converted to
H.264 for browser playback). The project owner explicitly authorized public
publication of the supplied studies on 2026-09-29. Local review sheets and
media-processing dependencies remain git-ignored. This authorization is not a
new third-party redistribution license; retain provenance and source credits.
